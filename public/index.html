    How it works

    Each equipment line becomes an average power by tier. The outage is one continuous block per day (the worst case for storage). Solar follows a half-sine from 06:00 to 18:00 that sums to the peak sun hours. During grid hours the grid carries the hospital and recharges the battery. During the outage, each hour serves tier 1, then tier 2, then tier 3 from solar, then battery, and the generator only covers tiers 1 and 2. The simulation runs three days and reports the third, once the battery state has settled.

    Auto-sizing makes solar produce the daily outage energy of tiers 1 and 2, the battery hold one full outage block of tiers 1 and 2, and the generator back up tier 1 for cloudy days. Open source (MIT) on GitHub, by Design Master Solutions LLC.
