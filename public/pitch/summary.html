Hospital Uptime

Tiered solar, battery and generator plans that keep a hospital's life-critical loads on through 8 to 20 hours of daily grid outage.

Problem

Nigerian hospitals lose grid power 8-20 hours a day. Most run every load on one diesel genset for every outage hour: high fuel cost, dependence on fuel supply, and a single point of failure for oxygen, vaccine fridges and ICU equipment.

Solution

  • Equipment is listed by tier: 1 life-critical (never shed), 2 essential, 3 comfort (shed first, never on diesel).
  • The app sizes solar, battery and a small generator for tiers 1-2, simulates every hour, stress-tests 8-20 h outages on sunny and cloudy days, and shows cost, monthly payment, diesel saved and payback.
  • The printed plan goes to an installer for quotes and to a lender for financing.

Results (sample hospital, 14 h outage)

Life-critical unserved0 kWh
Diesel21 → 2.5 L/day
Payback4.3 years

All 8 stress cases keep tiers 1-2 on; diesel 0-4.4 L/day; only comfort load is shed.
